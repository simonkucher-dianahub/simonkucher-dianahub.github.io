FactorStrat

Find the drivers behind change in revenue or margin

Download Installer

Description

FactorStrat breaks down the change in revenue or margin between two periods into the drivers behind it, such as price, volume, mix and cost. It turns raw transaction data into a clear bridge that shows how much each factor contributed, so teams can see what actually moved the numbers and where to act.
