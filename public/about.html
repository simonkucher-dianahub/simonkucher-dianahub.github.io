Hello from Diana!

We're a team of tech-minded consultants based in Latin America. Our goal is to make analysis faster, smarter, and deeper. Through our project work, we've built tools and methods that uncover sharper insights and automate tasks that aren't easily done in Excel. We're sharing them with the whole Simon-Kucher family to hopefully help your projects run a little smoother and aim a little higher.

Our name stands for Digital Analytics, and it's also a nod to La Diana Cazadora, the iconic Huntress statue in Mexico City, who's been taking aim since 1942.
